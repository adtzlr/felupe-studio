# Meshes of the example ex07 of FElupe

The meshes of the engine mount of the example `ex07_engine-mount.py` of
[FElupe](https://github.com/adtzlr/felupe/tree/main/examples) (GPL-3.0), unchanged:
`mesh-metal.vtk`, `mesh-rubber.vtk` and `mesh-air.vtk` (originally
`ex07_engine-mount_mesh-*.vtk`). `make_samples.py` writes them as the VTU files of
the sample _Engine mount_ (#241), the cells of the metal tagged as the inner (1) and
the outer part (2) by the radius of their centers.
