# 0020 Brands: own deployments

Status: Accepted

## Context

An institute or a company wants to run FElupe Studio under its own name, e.g. on its own GitLab instance or on an intranet without internet access. It wants its own samples and links, and it wants to keep following the releases of felupe-studio (#321). A fork drifts from the releases. Today:

- **Name and links:** the name is a constant (`APP_NAME` in `app/src/app.ts`), in `index.html` and in a few texts; the links are constants (`LINKS`).
- **Icons:** the app has no favicon, no icons and no manifest.
- **Samples:** one folder per build (`SAMPLES_DIR`), whose list the app loads at runtime (`samples/index.json`, #350).
- **Pyodide:** it comes from `cdn.jsdelivr.net` (`pyodide.worker.ts`), and the service worker caches it by that URL (`public/sw.js`).
- **The browser storage has fixed names:**
  - the keys `felupe-studio.*` of `localStorage` (the contour, the curve, the live results, the picking) and of `sessionStorage` (the token of `serve`, the agent);
  - the session `felupe-studio` in IndexedDB;
  - the bytecode cache `/pycache` (IDBFS, ADR 0017);
  - the cache `felupe-studio-runtime-v1` of the service worker.
- **The local runtime:** `felupe-studio serve` serves the app of its wheel (ADR 0014), and the wheel has the samples of the build (#350).

## Decision

- **A brand is a folder, given at build time:** `BRAND_DIR=~/my-brand npm run build` (also `npm run dev`). Without it, the build takes the default brand `app/brand/`, which is felupe-studio itself. The folder has a `brand.json` and its files: the logo, the icons and the samples. A JSON schema validates `brand.json` in the plugin of Vite, and an invalid brand fails the build with the message, like a folder of samples (#350). `SAMPLES_DIR` beside `BRAND_DIR` is an error, so the brand folder is the whole configuration of a deployment.
- **What a brand sets** in its `brand.json`:
  - `name` and `short_name`: the title, the header, the welcome panel, the texts of the app and the manifest;
  - `logo` (SVG, the header and the favicon), `icons` (PNG of 192 and 512 px, the manifest), `accent` (a color: `--color-accent` and the theme color of the manifest) and `welcome` (the text below the title of the welcome panel);
  - `links`: `help`, `documentation`, `tutorial`, `issues` and `source`. The GPL asks for the source of the deployed version: the release tag of felupe-studio and the brand;
  - `samples`: the folder of its samples, relative to the brand folder (#350); without it, the brand has no samples;
  - `pyodide`: `cdn` (the default) or `self-hosted`;
  - `storage`: the prefix of the browser storage;
  - `defaults.units`: the units of a new model (`model.settings`, #132).
- **A brand inherits no identity from upstream:** `name`, `short_name`, `logo`, `storage` and the links `help`, `issues` and `source` are required and have no defaults. So a missing link never sends the issues of a company upstream, and a change upstream never changes a brand. Only technical options have defaults: Pyodide from the CDN, no samples, the units of today.
- **The build writes the brand into static files:**
  - `index.html`: the title, the favicon, the link of the manifest, the theme color, and the brand as a JSON block (`<script type="application/json" id="brand">`). This block is the one source of the brand in the app: `app/src/brand.ts` reads it before the other modules use it, and `APP_NAME` and `LINKS` come from it;
  - `manifest.webmanifest` with the name, the short name, the icons and the colors;
  - the folder of samples with its list (#350), and with `self-hosted` the files of Pyodide.

  The app fetches no `brand.json`. The service worker gets the prefix of its cache from the URL that registers it (`sw.js?storage=<prefix>`, none for FElupe Studio), so `sw.js` stays a static file.
- **felupe-studio is the default brand** (`app/brand/brand.json`). It gives the values of today, so the settings and the sessions of existing users stay, and nothing is migrated. A test checks the title, the keys, the session, `/pycache` and the cache of the service worker. Every build of `main` builds a brand.
- **The identity of upstream is no brand.** These are constants of the code, the same in every brand:
  - *based on FElupe Studio &lt;version&gt;* in *About*, with the link to the upstream project and the license (GPL-3.0-or-later); the default brand shows no *based on*;
  - the header of the generated script (*generated by FElupe Studio &lt;version&gt; (command schema N)*), for traceability;
  - the Python package `felupe-studio`, its module and its CLI, the texts of the Python side (the CLI, the MCP server) and the meta tags of `serve`.
- **The storage of a brand:**
  - the keys `<storage>.<name>` in `localStorage` and `sessionStorage`;
  - the session `<storage>` in IndexedDB;
  - the bytecode cache `/pycache` for the prefix `felupe-studio`, else `/<storage>-pycache`;
  - the cache `<storage>-runtime-v1` of the service worker.

  So two brands on one origin keep their data apart, e.g. two folders of the Pages of one GitLab group, or two `serve` on `127.0.0.1:8765` one after the other. The modules take their keys from `storageKey(name)` of `app/src/brand.ts`.
- **Self-hosted Pyodide:** with `pyodide: "self-hosted"`, the build copies Pyodide into `dist/pyodide/`:
  - the core of the npm package `pyodide`, which is pinned like today;
  - the packages that the app loads (`numpy`, `scipy`, `rich` and their dependencies in `pyodide-lock.json`), downloaded once at build time and checked against the SHA-256 of the lock file.

  The worker gets the URL of Pyodide from the app, and the service worker caches it like the wheels. The build needs internet access, the deployment does not.
- **The wheel of a brand:** the Python package built after the build of the brand (`python -m build`) has the app and the samples of the brand (#350), without Pyodide, since `serve` runs CPython. It is installed from its file, e.g. `pip install "felupe_studio-0.8.0-py3-none-any.whl[serve,mcp]"`. It keeps the version of the release, and *About* shows the brand. A package index of a brand is not planned.
- **The repository of a brand** holds only the brand folder and a CI file:
  - the templates for GitLab CI and GitHub Actions (`brands/`) check out a release tag of felupe-studio and build it with the brand folder;
  - they deploy `app/dist` to Pages, and optionally keep the wheel as a file of the pipeline.

  An example brand (`brands/example/`, with samples of its own and a self-hosted Pyodide) is built in CI and checked with Playwright: the title, the manifest, the samples, and no request to the CDN.
- **Projects stay compatible:** a brand changes neither the command schema, nor the project files, nor the materials. A project of one brand opens in every other brand and upstream, with the same or a newer version; an older version says that the project may be of a newer one (#344). The samples of a brand are ordinary projects.
- **Switches:** none in v0.8. A later switch (e.g. *no mesh scripts*) is a constant of the build that leaves out the code. A project with what a switch turns off still opens, with an issue instead of an error of the schema, since projects of other brands and of the MCP server may use it.
- **Agents:** the agent bridge needs `serve --agent` (ADR 0015), so a brand on Pages has none, and the wheel of a brand has it. The MCP server (`felupe-studio-mcp`) stays that of upstream; its samples are those of its package or of `FELUPE_STUDIO_SAMPLES`. There is no hosted MCP server: it would run the mesh scripts of foreign projects (ADR 0014).

## Consequences

- The names and the links of the app come from one module, `app/src/brand.ts`, instead of constants. The tests run with the default brand.
- The keys of the browser storage are made by one function, and new keys (e.g. of #335) use it.
- The build has a second plugin, `brand()`, next to `samples()`.
- `brand.json`, `BRAND_DIR` and the templates are an interface for others. `brand.json` has a `brand_version` (1). An addition keeps it; a change that is not additive raises it, and the build says what to change.
- Upstream maintains the templates and the example brand, and CI builds the example at every change.
- A self-hosted Pyodide makes a deployment larger by tens of megabytes (the core, NumPy and SciPy).

## Alternatives

- **The released wheel branded at runtime** (`felupe-studio serve --brand <folder>`): no Node, no build, and the tested artifact. But it brands only the local runtime, not a hosted page for many users, also on phones. Its switches are no policies, since a user may start it without `--brand`. And the app needs its brand at runtime anyway. It stays a candidate (#349): with the JSON block of `index.html`, `serve` would only replace the block and the static files.
- **A static copy made from the wheel** (`felupe-studio brand <folder> --out public/`): it has the drawbacks of both ways. The wheel has no wheels of Pyodide, and the name is compiled into the bundle.
- **A `brand.json` fetched at runtime:** the title, the manifest and the icons would be wrong until it loads, an invalid brand would fail in the browser of a user, and a self-hosted Pyodide needs a build anyway.
- **A fork:** it drifts from the releases. The GPL allows it, but a brand folder is less work for both sides.
- **Themes and plugins:** beyond an accent color, not in v0.8.
